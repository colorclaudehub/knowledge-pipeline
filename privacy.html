Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Knowledge Pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope youtube.readonly), it reads the titles and video lists of the owner's own YouTube playlists. It cannot change anything on YouTube.

How the data is used and stored

The playlist data is used only to create notes in the owner's private knowledge base. It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Knowledge Pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
